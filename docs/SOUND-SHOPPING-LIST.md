# SOUND SHOPPING LIST

Where to look: **YouTube Studio → Audio Library → Sound Effects tab**
(studio.youtube.com, free, no attribution required). If it is thin on anything
below, **pixabay.com/sound-effects** and **freesound.org** cover the same ground
and are also free.

**Two rules that matter more than which file you pick.** Favour **short** over
long: anything above 1.5 seconds will outstay its welcome in cuts this tight.
And favour **low** over bright: the sounds you disliked were bright sweeps, and
that is the part that grates. If a sound has a lot of hiss or air in it, skip it.

Grab **2 or 3 options per category** so we can A/B rather than committing blind.
Around 12 files total is plenty. Name them by category (`section_1.wav`,
`review_2.wav`) and I will slot them straight in.

## 4. REVIEWS — the client proof wall, 1:04 to 1:21

Twelve reviews arrive. Only the **first four** get a sound, at falling volume,
and then they stop, so the rest accumulate in silence under the music. That is
deliberate and it is what stops it sounding like an inbox.

| At | What is happening |
|---|---|
| 1:05 | the Rising Talent badge, the first accent |
| 1:06 / 1:08 / 1:08 / 1:09 | the first four reviews, each quieter than the last |
| 1:11 | the centre review, where the camera nearly stops for 2.3 seconds |

**What it needs to do:** feel like something being *placed*, not like a
notification. This is the easiest category to get wrong.

**Search:** `soft click` · `tick subtle` · `paper place` · `card flip soft` ·
`key press`

**Avoid at all costs:** anything with `notification`, `pop`, `ding`, `alert`,
`message`, or `bubble` in the name. One of those and the whole wall reads as a
phone going off.

For the **centre review at 1:11**, which gets its own moment, something slightly
warmer and pitched: `bell soft` · `tonal swell short`

**Length:** under 0.3 seconds for the four, up to 1 second for the centre one.
Get 2 plus 1.
