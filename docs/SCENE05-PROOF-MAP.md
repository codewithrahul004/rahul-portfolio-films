Twelve pieces of evidence, entering on an uneven rhythm while the camera falls
backwards.

| Frames | Seconds | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 22 | 0.0 – 0.7 | Scene 04's last surface, Edit Lobby, is still in frame and becomes the Upwork profile in place. No cut. |
| 22 – 50 | 0.7 – 1.7 | One push onto `Rising Talent ★★★★★ 5.0 (3)`. The doorway, not the proof. |
| 58 – 190 | 1.9 – 6.3 | Five public ones arrive: two Upwork reviews, Instagram, Google, Instagram. |
| 198 – 268 | 6.6 – 8.9 | **Basavaraj lands dead centre and the camera nearly stops on it for 2.3 seconds.** |
| 254 – 355 | 8.5 – 11.8 | The remaining chats arrive from five directions, two of them into the far layer. |

| 408 – 492 | 13.6 – 16.4 | The wall drops to 20%, the camera keeps easing back, and THE WORK SPEAKS. / THE CLIENTS CONFIRM IT. holds for 1.2s before the push into Scene 06. |

### The centre card

"First off, the landing page looks incredible and is already performing beyond
expectations" is the only piece of feedback in the set that reports a business
outcome rather than a feeling, so it now sits at the centre of the finished
wall and gets the longest look in the scene. At that moment it is 520px wide
on a 1920 frame, which puts the client's own words at 27px. Readable, not
skimmed.

### Highlighting, without touching the screenshot

Each chat card now lights the sentence that matters and stops the rest of the
same thread down to 34% with a little desaturation, with a thin green rule
down the left of the lit region. Both layers are the identical image at the
identical size, so nothing is cropped, moved, re-typeset or hidden — the whole
conversation stays on screen and legible, the eye is just told where to land.
The highlight lifts eight frames after the card settles, so you read the
thread first and the line second.

Lit lines, in order of appearance:

- **Basavaraj** — "First off, the landing page looks incredible and is already
  performing beyond expectations. Really appreciate the work you've put into it."
- **Guneet** — "Omg / Its perfect / I love it / Thank you so much / It looks perfect"
- **Anisha** — "Lots of great feedback on the website! It's looking very good -
  simple yet impactful"
- **Mustafa** — "looks great"
- **Guneet** — "I love it so much / Its so beautiful"
- **Anisha** — "just a quick update that your sitelinks are visible in google
  search result." / "Amazing!"

### Names

Every chat and social card now carries the client's name and the platform in
the film's own type, under the card: Basavaraj, Guneet, Anisha, Mustafa on
whatsapp; Avneet Makhni and Skinfluence on instagram; Sameer Gautam on google

Captions are sized against the card so they support the evidence rather than
compete with it.

### Motion

Each card enters with scale 0.96 → 1.00, opacity 0 → 1, a masked reveal and a
directional move of 56 to 92 pixels, over 13 to 20 frames, with directions and
durations varied per card. No bounce, no elastic, no rotation. The camera is
Scene 04's single monotone-cubic path, easeOutCubic and easeInOutCubic only,
no springs; fades that start from a standing hold use the S-curve so the
opacity ramp has no step in its velocity.

**16.4 seconds**, up from 14.0. The extra 2.4 are the near-hold on the centre
card and a longer readable beat on the closing line, both of which you asked
for. Verified on the render: 1920x1080, `r_frame_rate = avg_frame_rate =
30/1`, 492 frames, no black frames, two frozen frames and no frozen run, and
the only sharp frame-to-frame change is the badge arriving.
